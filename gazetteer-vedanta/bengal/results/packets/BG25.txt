### BG25 Darjeeling (in.ernet.dli.2015.206868.txt)

--- p.135 @359563
  In Darjeeling itself the commonest conveyances for those who do not ride are the luxurious rickshaw and the hill dandy.
* The latter is a long ooffin-like reclining chair with one end resting on the shoulder of a bearer, and the other slung across a pole (dandi) which rests on the shoulders of two men behind.
  For long journeys four men are necessary, and then the dandy iB supported on poles hoth before and behind.

### BG15 Chittagong (dli.ministry.07913.txt)

--- p.88 @104466
  The Tipperas believe in a hereafter, a pleasant land where those that have done good will live in ease. and comfort, and where both sexes have equal rights: a land of barrenness where ceaseless toil produces no results, and constant harassment by “ bhuts ” awaits the evil-doer.
* The Tipperas have a class of Bairagis amongst themselver, and these people never do a stroke of work, living solely by begging and the singing of religious songs.
  The Tipperas are exceedingly superstitious; it is exceedingly unlucky for a kite to settle on the roof of the house, for a dog to jump up on the roof, or for a crow to sit and caw on the roof in the early morning.

In Gujarat, Bengal, the Punjab, the Central Provinces, Mathura, Ayodhyā and the Liṅgāyat Karnatak, Vaiṣṇava, Nāth, Udāsī, Bairāgī or Liṅgāyat bodies held the larger followings.
